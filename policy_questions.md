## Notes

### Key Survey Design Features:
1. **7-point scales**: Many policy questions use 7-point scales with endpoints labeled (e.g., V201255, V201258, V201262, V201429)
2. **Follow-up strength questions**: Many favor/oppose questions include follow-ups asking about intensity (e.g., "strongly" or "not strongly")
3. **Candidate placement**: Several policy scales ask respondents to place both themselves and the candidates (Biden/Trump) on the same scale
4. **Timing**: Some questions are pre-election only, while others are post-election only (marked as POST-ELECTION)

### Standard Response Codes:
- -9: Refused
- -8: Don't know
- -7: No post-election data (for post-election questions)
- -6: No post-election interview
- -5: Interview breakoff
- -4: Technical error
- -1: Inapplicable
- 99: Haven't thought much about this (for 7-point scales)
