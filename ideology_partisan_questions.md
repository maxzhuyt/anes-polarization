## Notes

### Standard 7-Point Party ID Scale
The standard 7-point party identification scale is constructed using:
- **V201228** (primary party ID)
- **V201229** (party strength for Democrats/Republicans)
- **V201230** (leaning for Independents)

This yields the following categories:
1. Strong Democrat
2. Weak Democrat
3. Independent-leaning Democrat
4. Independent
5. Independent-leaning Republican
6. Weak Republican
7. Strong Republican

### Usage Recommendations
- **Partisan affiliation**: Use V201228, V201229, and V201230 to construct the standard 7-point party ID scale
- **Ideology**: Use V201200 as the primary measure of self-reported ideology on the liberal-conservative dimension
- **Perceptual measures**: V201202-V201205 provide respondents' perceptions of where candidates and parties fall on the ideological spectrum, useful for measuring perceived ideological distance
